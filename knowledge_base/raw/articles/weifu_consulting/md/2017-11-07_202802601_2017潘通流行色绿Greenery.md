# 韦孚色彩趋势｜2017潘通流行色 绿 “Greenery Stole The Show”

> 来源: https://www.sohu.com/a/202802601_100049954
> 抓取日期: 2026-08-25

Greenery (the colour, not foliage or vegetation) has been named the Pantone Colour of Year for 2017;the hue of hope and growth.
绿色(颜色，并非绿叶或植物）被潘通选为2017的代表色，一种代表着希望及成长的色调。
Some might say the concept behind Pantone's prophecy is a rather self-fulfilling one, but we embrace it all the same. (Not to mention, we predicted it back in the October 2016 issue.)
有些人可能会说，潘通的预言无法代表大部分人，这是一种极为自我的见解。但我们与潘通一样如此认为。（更不用说我们早在2016年10月号的杂志中就曾经提到过。）
Fashion's love of so-bad-it's-good geekiness dictated an unlikely new power colour in the spring/summer 2017 shows.
时尚对于这种“靠这也太棒了”的古怪感的热衷暗示了17 ss出现的新流行色。
Making that vivid, verdant shade a real scene-stealer. See Vogue's edit of the best looks to utilise greenery here.
植物般翠绿的灵动，融入进真实的场景。请看《VOGUE》精选的运用绿色的最佳时装秀looks。
